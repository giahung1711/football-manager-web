FOOTBALL MANAGER - MARKET LIMITS, ELIGIBILITY AND RESET

- Replaced the settings navigation sun-like glyph with a clear horizontal sliders icon.
- Replaced the transfer-market zig-zag glyph with a storefront icon.
- Each player may have at most five active player-to-player market listings.
  The limit is enforced in both single and bulk listing endpoints.
- Eligibility now uses the player's original/base OVR:
  * Base OVR 100-101 requires upgrade level +7 or higher.
  * Base OVR 102 or higher requires upgrade level +5 or higher.
  * Existing lower-OVR eligibility rules are retained.
- The player's inventory/listing form is displayed as compact searchable and sortable rows.
- Added an admin-only marketplace reset action in Admin Panel. It marks all active listings
  as withdrawn and returns each listed card to its original seller's saved inventory group
  (bench/extras). Already-sold listings and PC/PG balances are not modified.
- The reset is an explicit admin action after deployment; it is not run automatically at startup.
