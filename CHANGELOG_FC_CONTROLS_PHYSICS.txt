FOOTBALL MANAGER 3D - FC STYLE CONTROL UPDATE

- Added mobile action buttons: short pass, through ball, long/high pass, shoot, tackle, sprint.
- Added keyboard mappings J/I/O/K/L and Shift sprint.
- Added player acceleration, deceleration and turning inertia.
- Added ball gravity, bounce, rolling friction and spin.
- Added basic player body collision.
- Kept the tactical wide pitch and fixed-angle lateral-follow camera from the previous build.

This is an arcade-style prototype, not a full FC Online physics/animation engine. Three.js is still loaded from jsDelivr CDN.
